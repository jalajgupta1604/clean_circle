# Master Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Product Name: CleanCircle (Working Title)

Category: ClimateTech / Urban Infrastructure SaaS Business Model:
Prepaid subscription + usage-based waste collection Primary Market:
Urban India (Tier 1 & Tier 2 cities)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. Product Vision

Create a data-driven waste management ecosystem that: - Incentivizes
households to reduce waste - Makes waste collection transparent -
Introduces fair pricing based on waste generation - Digitizes waste
operations for cities

Ultimate vision: Build the Urban Waste Operating System for Indian
cities.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. Problem Statement

Current waste collection systems suffer from:

1.  No accountability -- users don't know if garbage was actually
    collected.
2.  No data -- municipalities lack reliable metrics on waste generation.
3.  Flat pricing -- households producing less waste pay the same as
    heavy waste producers.
4.  No incentives -- there is no reward for reducing waste.
5.  Operational inefficiency -- collectors follow inefficient routes.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4. Product Components

The platform contains three applications:

1.  Customer App -- household waste management portal
2.  Agent App -- collection confirmation and route management
3.  Admin Platform -- operations and analytics dashboard

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 5. Product Development Phases

## Phase 1 --- MVP (Core Platform)

Goal: Launch pilot in one society.

### Feature: User Registration

Users register using: - Google OAuth - Mobile OTP - Address selection

Why: Reduces signup friction and maps household to physical location.

### Feature: Household Profile

Stores: - Address - Family size - Bucket plan - Wallet balance

Why: Enables future analytics and prediction of waste generation.

### Feature: Bucket-Based Subscription

Example:

  Plan       Bucket Size   Monthly Price
  ---------- ------------- ---------------
  Basic      10L           ₹199
  Standard   25L           ₹349
  Premium    50L           ₹599

Why: Encourages waste reduction and fair pricing.

### Feature: Prepaid Wallet

Recharge via: - UPI - Card - Net Banking

Each pickup deducts wallet balance.

Why: Eliminates payment defaults and simplifies billing.

### Feature: Pickup Confirmation

Agent confirms collection using: - QR scan - GPS tagging - Timestamp

Why: Prevents fake pickup reporting and billing disputes.

### Feature: Waste Tracking Dashboard

Shows: - Pickup history - Estimated waste volume - Monthly trend

Why: Visibility encourages behavioral change.

### Feature: Agent Mobile App

Includes: - Route list - Household list - Pickup confirmation - Missed
pickup mark - Offline sync

Why: Digitizes field operations.

### Feature: Admin Dashboard

Allows: - Agent management - Route creation - Pricing configuration -
Revenue monitoring - Pickup monitoring

Why: Enables operational scaling.

### Feature: Notification System

Alerts for: - Pickup confirmation - Low wallet balance - Missed pickups

Why: Builds transparency and trust.

### Feature: QR Household Identification

Each household gets QR sticker.

Why: Prevents incorrect pickup reporting.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Phase 2 --- Growth Features

### Reward System

Users earn rewards for: - Waste reduction - Segregation - Consistent
participation

Why: Gamification drives behavior change.

### Waste Reduction Analytics

Displays reduction trends and impact metrics.

Why: Motivates sustainability behavior.

### Referral Program

Users earn wallet credit for inviting neighbors.

Why: Lowers acquisition cost.

### Society Dashboard

Provides waste analytics per building.

Why: Societies want sustainability insights.

### Route Optimization

Algorithm suggests efficient collection routes.

Why: Reduces fuel and operational cost.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Phase 3 --- Smart Waste Platform

### Smart Weight Integration

Vehicle weighing sensors measure actual waste weight.

Why: Enables precise billing and analytics.

### AI Waste Prediction

Predicts high waste areas and seasonal spikes.

Why: Improves route planning and efficiency.

### Carbon Impact Score

Displays environmental impact of waste reduction.

Why: Encourages eco-friendly participation.

### Recycling Marketplace

Users sell recyclables such as paper, plastic, and metal.

Why: Creates circular economy opportunities.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Phase 4 --- Municipal SaaS Platform

### Ward-Level Dashboard

Municipal officials view waste per ward.

### Contractor Performance Monitoring

Evaluates contractors on pickup completion and efficiency.

### Compliance Reports

Generates reports aligned with national cleanliness initiatives.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Phase 5 --- Urban Sustainability Platform

### Green Credit Score

Measures environmental behavior of households.

### Property Tax Incentives

Cities provide rebates for eco-friendly homes.

### ESG Reporting

Corporations sponsor waste reduction programs.
